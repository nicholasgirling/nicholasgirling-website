    Recently released. This piece has remained in the artists own living space since before covid and beyond only made available in 2026 July. It is part of a series called 'Organics'and was repainted a number of times. It is perhaps the most popular piece from that series. An important artwork framed in a rustic blackwood, it represents the result of experimentation in exploring an innate style for the artist. Painting without a preconceived notion of what he would create. After three years of exploration, Nicholas uncovered multiple styles. often those styles were mostly informed by a state of mind. The interesting thing for the artist is that these paintings are monochromatic or limited in colours whcih solves the complexity issue. If they were using multiple colours and used the same complexity of form, they would be overly complex. But being more muted and more peaceful, he has been able to find both peace and complexity. Take this piece as an insight into the mind of an artist full of ideas and at peace with that, Over time, the artist will continue to raise the price on this important piece of art that signalled to him that he was onto something. not for the viewer, but for himself.
